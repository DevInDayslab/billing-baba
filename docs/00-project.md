# Project

## Overview

**Billing Baba** is offline-first POS, billing, and business management software. It targets small and medium businesses that need reliable billing on desktop and mobile, with cloud sync for backup and multi-device use.

## Objectives

- Deliver a fast, offline-capable POS and billing experience on desktop and mobile
- Enable businesses to manage products, customers, and invoices from a single system
- Provide secure cloud backup and synchronization across devices

## Scope

### In Scope

- POS and invoicing
- Product and inventory management
- Customer management
- Business reports
- Offline-first operation with cloud sync
- Desktop (Windows, macOS) and mobile (Android, iOS) clients
- Fastify REST API for authentication, sync, and backup
